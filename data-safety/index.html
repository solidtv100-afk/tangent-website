Data Safety

Last updated 25 October 2026 · Mirrors the shipped app build for com.tangent.app

No data is collected or shared off-device. Tangent stores records on the device and does not use a remote service, analytics SDK, ads, or a cloud account.

What the app stores locally

Tangent keeps the data it needs for your fitness and wellness history on your device, including:

  • Workout progress, exercise logs, and plan history.
  • Walking, movement, sleep, and custom log entries.
  • Body metrics, health checks, and progress history.
  • Food and water logs, hydration history, saved custom foods, and nutrition settings.
  • Breathing, fasting, gratitude, afternoon/evening routines, and relationship skill progress.
  • App settings such as reminder configuration and optional app-lock setup.

Device permissions used

The app requests the following device permissions for on-device features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS for reminders.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC for the optional fingerprint app lock.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION for step and activity tracking.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION for walking/location features.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION for walking and reminder foreground services.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED to restore reminders after reboot.

The app does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or other unrelated permissions.
