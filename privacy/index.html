Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.tangent.app

Short version: Tangent stores your records locally on your device and does not transmit them anywhere. It has no user account, no cloud sync, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no internet permission. Everything you enter remains in the app's private storage on the device you use.

1. Who is responsible

Tangent Fitness & Wellness is developed and published by MD AYUB MONDAL, an individual developer. For any privacy question, write to solid.tv.100@gmail.com.

2. What Tangent does not do

These statements describe the app as built, and each one can be checked against the app's manifest and dependency list:

  • It does not request the INTERNET permission. Without that permission Android refuses any network request the app might attempt.
  • It has no user accounts. No registration, no email address or server-side profile.
  • It contains no advertising SDK.
  • It contains no analytics or telemetry SDK.
  • It contains no third-party crash-reporting service.
  • It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or file access.
  • It is not built around a remote backend. There is no cloud backup, no server sync and no account recovery system.

3. What Tangent stores, and where

The app stores the data it needs to show your history back to you. All of it lives in the app's private storage area on your own device. This can include:

  • Your display name, if you enter one, plus body metrics and plan preferences.
  • Workout progress, session history, challenge status, exercise logs, and sets or repetitions you record.
  • Walking and step data, daily movement history, and walk settings.
  • Food and water entries, saved custom foods, hydration history and hydration goals.
  • Morning routine progress, gratitude journal entries, vision board content, and other wellness progression data.
  • Health Check answers and results, including self-screening responses and screen calibration for vision-related checks.
  • Relationship Skills progress, reflections, daily check-ins and lesson history.
  • Settings such as interface language, haptics, rest duration, first day of the week, and app lock settings.
  • If you enable the app lock, a salted cryptographic hash of your PIN — never the PIN itself.

This information is not transmitted anywhere. There is no cloud sync and no backup server, which also means the app cannot restore your history if you move to a new phone unless you export a file yourself.

4. Android backup

The app excludes its database from Android backup and device-to-device transfer; your records are therefore not copied into your Google account by the operating system.

5. Permissions, and why each one exists

Every permission in the app's release manifest.
PermissionWhy it is neededOptional?
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows reminders you schedule yourself for workouts, fasting, hydration, routines and other local task timings. Yes
USE_BIOMETRIC Lets you unlock the optional app lock with a fingerprint instead of your PIN. Your biometric data is handled by Android and is never visible to the app. Yes — only if you turn the app lock on
VIBRATE Haptic feedback during workouts, timers and alerts. There is a setting to switch it off. Yes

The app does not request exact-alarm permissions; reminders are scheduled inexactly to save battery, and Android may deliver them a little late.

6. Third-party components

Tangent uses common Android and local-only libraries for UI, persistence and rendering. None of them collects or transmits personal information in this app in the current build:

  • AndroidX and Jetpack Compose
  • AndroidX Room and local data persistence
  • Kotlin Coroutines
  • SceneView / Filament for on-device rendering when the exercise animation system is enabled in a build

There is no Google Analytics, Firebase, ad SDK, or social-media SDK in the current app build.

7. When information leaves your device — only if you send it

Two things can move data off the device, and both require you to act:

  • Progress export. You can export a progress report as a PDF or text file, or save custom logs as CSV. The file is handed to Android's share sheet, where you choose the destination.
  • Contacting support. If you email solid.tv.100@gmail.com, your message and email address reach that mailbox, as with any email.

8. Spoken coaching and text-to-speech

Optional spoken coaching passes short phrases to the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. That engine is part of Android or an app you installed separately, not part of Tangent. Turn spoken coaching off if you would rather it were not used.

9. Crash reports

If the app crashes, it writes a technical report to a file in its own private storage and shows it the next time you open the app so you can send it to support if you want to. That file is never uploaded automatically.

10. Children

Tangent is not directed at children and is not designed for use by anyone under 13. Because the app keeps no server-side data, it holds no children's data on a backend.

11. Your rights over your data

Since your information stays on your handset, you already have complete control of it: there is no remote record to request, correct, export or erase. To remove everything, see Delete your data.

12. Security

Your records sit in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. You can add a PIN and fingerprint lock inside the app. Please also protect the device itself with a screen lock: on an unlocked device, anyone holding it can open most apps. We do not claim perfect security, only that this is the app's actual design.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app adds an online feature or a new third-party dependency, this page will be updated before that version ships. We will not quietly begin collecting data under an old policy.
