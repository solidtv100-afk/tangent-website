Reporting a problem

Please include:

  • What you were doing and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • The screen or feature involved.
  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The app version or Play Store listing details.
  • A screenshot if the issue is visible.

If the app crashed: reopen it and capture the technical crash report shown on the next launch. That report is stored only on your device and is not sent automatically.
