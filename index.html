Android · Offline-first

Train, eat, move and recover better — without an account.

Tangent is a local-first fitness and wellness app for Android. It brings together guided workouts, exercise guidance, routines, walking, hydration, nutrition tracking, health checks, and progress history — all without a connection or a cloud account.

Get access & support See what data it handles

Current feature status

Tangent is a local-first wellness app for everyday fitness, habit tracking, nutrition, hydration, walking, and progress. The app currently ships with the 3D character animation system turned off by design, while the exercise guidance and logging workflows remain available in text-first form.

✅

Available now

Guided workouts and challenge structure, exercise guidance and local exercise logging, routines, breathing, walking, hydration tracking, nutrition logging, health checks, relationship skills, gratitude, progress history, body metrics, and local export.

🚫

Disabled in this build

The 3D character animation stage is intentionally off in the current app build. The exercise library and workout guidance remain, but the character-based playback experience is not part of the shipped release.

📰

Unavailable or future

No cloud account, no online sync, no social feed, no barcode scanner, no camera-based food recognition, no ads, no analytics SDK, and no remote coaching. Any future online feature would require disclosure in the privacy and data-safety pages before shipping.

Built to work offline

Tangent does not request the Android internet permission. The app has no networking code and no network libraries compiled into it, so it cannot open a connection, upload your records or contact an analytics service. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device.

📸

No account, ever

There is no sign-up, no email address and no profile on a server. Open the app and start. An optional PIN and fingerprint lock is available, and it is checked on your device.

📊

No analytics or ads

No advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service. If the app crashes it writes a report to a local file and shows it to you on the next launch, so you can send it if you want to.

🔒

Permissions

Notifications for reminders, biometric unlock for the optional app lock, activity recognition and location for walking, and vibration for haptics. That is the active list in the current release.

What's inside

🏋

Workouts & challenges

Daily and specialized training plans, starts and session history, warm-up and cooldown structure, and progress tracking across workouts.

📖

Exercise guidance

A written exercise library and logging flow across categories, with local history and progress tracking. The 3D character playback system is off in the current release.

🍳

Nutrition & hydration

Food logging, saved custom foods, daily water intake tracking, hydration reminders, and goal-based intake management without a live backend.

📈

Walking & habits

Daily step tracking, walking logs, morning routines, gratitude, vision board, and habit streaks in the same local-first data store.

⏳

Breathing & wellness

Guided breathing, health checks, relationship skills, and self-tracked wellness routines on the device.

🎁

Progress you own

Body metrics, streaks, achievements, history, PDF/text export, and private local storage — never a remote profile or synced server record.

Availability

Tangent is in closed testing on Google Play and is not yet publicly released. If you would like to take part, email solid.tv.100@gmail.com from the Google account you use on your Android device and you will be sent a tester invitation.

The app runs on Android 7.0 (API level 24) and newer. Interface text is available in 11 languages; English and Bengali are complete and the others fall back to English where a phrase has not been translated yet.

Tangent is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and nothing in it is medical advice. Please read the Health & Fitness Disclaimer before you start exercising.
